Character Studio portrait textures — CC0 1.0 Universal

  female_complexion.png contains normalized pigment and lip detail from
  CutOff3D's Indian Female Skin (indian_skin2e.png), in the Skins 01 CC0 pack.
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/skins01.html

  male_complexion.png contains normalized pigment from Mindfront's Male
  African Middleage skin. Both complexion atlases use tangent-normal and
  surface detail from the same skin's _NRM and _SPEC maps in Skins 02 CC0.
  The atlases retain the head UV layout and store a separate eyelid mask.
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/skins02.html

  eye_stroma.png derives from eyes/materials/green_eye.png in the MakeHuman
  system assets. Its iris is reprojected and masked for per-character tinting.
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
