resources/audio/rain.flac contains excerpts of two stereo field recordings from Freesound.
Both recordings were released under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

soundrecorder7, "Light Rain"
https://freesound.org/people/soundrecorder7/sounds/167034/

rifualk, "Forest Rainstorm 01"
https://freesound.org/people/rifualk/sounds/648474/

The excerpts were trimmed to 16-second loops, crossfaded, filtered,
level-adjusted and resampled to 24 kHz stereo.
