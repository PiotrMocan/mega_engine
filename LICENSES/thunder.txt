The recordings in resources/audio/thunder.flac are derived from these field recordings.
All three authors released their recordings under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

seth-m, "big thunder clap"
https://freesound.org/people/seth-m/sounds/458015/

Fission9, "Thunder, Close"
https://freesound.org/people/Fission9/sounds/505113/

BlueDelta, "THUNDER long rumbling - no Rain - 4-CHANNEL-44kHz.wav"
https://freesound.org/people/BlueDelta/sounds/367702/

The engine bank contains three 16-second mono takes at 24 kHz. The first two use
the seth-m and Fission9 strikes, respectively, with different sections of the
BlueDelta recording for the trailing rumble. The third uses the BlueDelta roll.
The edits include trimming, filtering, mono downmix, level adjustment and fades.
